## Concept

The waveform extraction (`FfmpegWaveformService.cs:229-233`) read 116 MB, 13% of the 4K MP4, but in a seek-heavy
pattern: 179 MB in 5,633 reads, about 47 per second of media. On MKV/TS it reads the whole file. On a spinning disk
that pattern can stretch both the waveform and whatever shares the disk with it.

## Why later

The cost on an HDD is inferred. T-195 already makes the MP4 run 3x cheaper in CPU (0.358 s to 0.115 s).

**Built after T-205.** Its only trigger reads T-205's slow-disk bench, which T-205 builds (T-188 leaves the
HDD-class bench to T-205).

## Trigger

T-205's slow-disk bench shows the waveform as a measurable share of load or export time.
