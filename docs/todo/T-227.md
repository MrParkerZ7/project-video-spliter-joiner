## Concept

Observed at T-195's build (2026-10-05) with the bundled ffmpeg 7.1. A synthetic 6 s clip (testsrc 320x240, 30 fps,
libx264, 2 s GOP) was remuxed with `-c copy` to `.mkv` and `.ts`. Today's grab args (`-ss t -i in -frames:v 1 -vf
scale=160:-1`) returned:

| container | -ss 1 | -ss 3 |
|---|---|---|
| mp4 | the frame at 1 s | the frame at 3 s |
| mkv | the frame at 1 s | the frame at 3 s |
| ts (format start 1.443 s) | the **2 s keyframe** | the **4 s keyframe** |

So on MPEG-TS the hover picture is up to one GOP late, while SPEC-005 / ADR 0006 (as amended by T-195) describe the
seek as frame-accurate on MP4/MOV/MKV only. It looks like ffmpeg's mpegts seek lands after the target and the
accurate-seek trim cannot go back. This is not yet confirmed. (The acceptance review of T-195 confirmed the same on a
`src.ts`: unflagged `-ss 1` gives frame 60, `-ss 3` gives frame 120.)

## Design

Investigate first: confirm whether the cause is the seek landing point or `start_time` handling.

Recommended (star) if it is the seek: for TS-family inputs (`.ts`, `.m2ts`, `.mts`), input-seek about one GOP early and
output-seek the remainder. This is a bounded extra decode, and it reuses T-219's `StartTime` for the offset.

Alternative: accept and document it, so hover on TS is keyframe-accurate.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] **Repro first:** a real-ffmpeg test on a generated TS asserts that the grab at a mid-GOP second decodes to the
      same picture as the MP4 original at that second. It fails today.
- [ ] After the fix it passes, with no change to MP4/MOV/MKV token lists (SPEC-005 I2) and no change to the bench `mkv`
      numbers.
- [ ] SPEC-005 What/I2 and ADR 0006 are updated.
