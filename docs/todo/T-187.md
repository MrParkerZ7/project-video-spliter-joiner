# (note) Need improve perfomance for 4K video, because when try to split 4K video it's super slowly…

## 1 — As captured (verbatim — never edited)

> Need improve perfomance for 4K video, because when try to split 4K video it's super slowly. please check all join/spilt/bulk cut

## 2 — Clarification

**Read as:** make Split, Join and Bulk Cut fast on 4K files, starting where the waits really are, not where they
seem to be. Every claim below was measured with the app's own Core services on **synthetic 4K fixtures only** (no
user video was opened, listed or probed).

**What was found:**
- **Split's copy is already at disk speed** (the ffmpeg step of a 3-part 4K split runs at the raw `-c copy` floor).
  The wait around it is the keyframe scan, which reads **100% of the file** before a cut can snap and before Split
  can start, plus a metadata probe repeated at every step (0.27 s per probe on 4K H.264, never cached).
- **Bulk Cut's Exact cut is the worst wait:** its head re-encode seeks after `-i`, so it decodes from the start of the
  file — 22.8 s for a cut at 1:44, 131 s at 9:00 of a 10-minute 4K file. Input seek gives the same frames in ~5 s at
  any position, and x264 `veryfast` halves that again.
- **Join** probes every clip ~3 times per run; frame pictures and the waveform pay a stream-info decode each time
  (identical output 2–3× faster without it on MP4/MOV); cut-point pictures decode most of a GOP and show the frame
  up to 1 s before the cut.
- **Correctness bugs found on the way:** an Exact cut on an **HEVC** source writes a file that is corrupt after the
  joint; an **H.264** Exact cut starts the kept part ~80 ms late with audio overlap; and **lossless** parts on B-frame
  and TS sources can start one GOP late (found at plan review).
