## Concept
Make cut selection visual: a timeline strip under the player showing the playhead, every cut marker (at its snapped position), and every detected candidate (colored by kind) — where clicking a position drops a cut there and clicking a marker/candidate seeks to it. This is the "select the timing to cut from the player" surface.

## Design
- A `TimelineView` (UserControl) bound to a `TimelineViewModel` (or projected properties on SplitViewModel): total `Duration`, current `Position` (playhead), and two projected collections of **normalized positions** (0..1 = time/Duration): `MarkerTicks` (snapped cut positions) + `CandidateTicks` (detected, with Kind→brush).
- Rendering: a horizontal track (Canvas/Grid) width-mapped; playhead line at `Position/Duration`; marker ticks + candidate ticks placed by their normalized position. Click on the track → map click-X → time → `SplitViewModel.AddCutAt(time)` (T-013's single entry point). Click a tick → seek (SeekToMarker / PreviewCandidate).
- Keep the MVP simple: ticks on a track + playhead. Drag-to-move a marker is a nice-to-have (optional; only if cheap).

## Detail
- Pure mapping functions (unit-testable, no WPF): `double ToNormalized(TimeSpan t, TimeSpan duration)` and `TimeSpan FromNormalized(double x, TimeSpan duration)` (clamp 0..1). These power both rendering and click→time.
- Markers/candidates update live as they're added/detected (observable → re-project ticks).
- Color candidates by Kind (Black/White/Scene) with a small legend; markers a distinct style.
- Place `TimelineView` directly beneath `PlayerView` in `SplitView`; it shares the same Duration/Position as the player.

## Interfaces / contracts
- Consumes: `Duration`/`Position` from the player, `Markers`/`Candidates` from SplitViewModel, and T-013's `AddCutAt(TimeSpan)` + seek commands.
- Produces: the visual cut-selection surface (no new engine contract).

## Acceptance criteria + Case-Coverage Matrix
- [ ] `ToNormalized`/`FromNormalized` are inverse within rounding; clamp out-of-range; handle Duration==0 safely (no divide-by-zero → 0).
- [ ] Given markers/candidates + a duration, the VM projects correct normalized tick positions (unit-tested).
- [ ] A timeline click at normalized x → AddCutAt(FromNormalized(x)) → a snapped marker appears (via T-013).
- [ ] Clicking a marker tick seeks the player to the marker; a candidate tick previews it.
- [ ] TimelineView compiles + integrates into SplitView; existing tests stay green.
- Matrix — Required-Success: click at x → cut at the right time (snapped). Boundary: click at x=0 and x=1 (start/end); Duration=0 (no ticks, no crash); many ticks (no overlap crash). Required-Fail: click before a file is loaded → no-op.

## Edge cases
Duration zero/unknown; click before load; ticks at identical positions; very short vs very long videos (tick density).

## Dependencies
T-012 (player Position/Duration), T-013 (AddCutAt + seek commands, reused).

## Risks
Pixel/time mapping off-by-one → cover with the pure inverse-function tests. Tick crowding on long videos → acceptable for MVP.

## Out of scope
Zoomable timeline, thumbnails/waveform, drag-trim handles — later.
