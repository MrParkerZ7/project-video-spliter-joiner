## Concept
Document the G-038 additions and keep the living specs current: USER_GUIDE + CHANGELOG, and extend the affected
`docs/specs/` specs with the new invariants (so `todo-automate` coverage stays honest).

## Design
1. **`docs/USER_GUIDE.md`** — in the Bulk Cut / cut-profiles section: profiles can carry a **thumbnail** (auto-defaults to
   the intro-end frame on save; **Upload image…** to override, **Clear** to remove) shown in the profile picker; and each
   row shows **frame thumbnails at its cut points** (intro-end / outro-start) to verify the cut visually.
2. **`CHANGELOG.md`** — an entry under Unreleased: "Bulk Cut — profile thumbnails + per-row cut-point frame previews (G-038)".
3. **Spec update (living-spec currency — the `serves-spec` contract):**
   - **SPEC-007 (cut-profiles):** add invariants for `ThumbnailPath` (optional; round-trip backward-compat; store
     Save/Delete/cascade; safe-name) — grounded in the shipped T-106.
   - **SPEC-011 (bulk-cut-screen):** add invariants for the per-row cut-point thumbnails (grab-at-snapped-time, debounce/
     coalesce, best-effort placeholder, outro thumb gated on HasOutro, bounded concurrency) — grounded in shipped T-108;
     and the profile-thumbnail UI (auto-default capture, upload/clear, picker display) from T-107.
   - Refresh `docs/specs/_index.md` invariant counts; the new tests (from T-106/107/108) carry `serves-spec:` so coverage
     stays ~current (note any that land in the deferred set).
4. **ARCHITECTURE.md** (optional, brief) — the `ProfileThumbnailStore` + the reuse of `IThumbnailService` for cut-point thumbs.
