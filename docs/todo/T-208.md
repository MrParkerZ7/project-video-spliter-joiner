## Concept

A hover grab decodes from the previous keyframe up to the hovered second, 0.50-0.62 s on 4K and 1.74-2.03 s mid-GOP
on a 10 s GOP. Snapping the hover to a keyframe the probe already knows would decode one frame. `-skip_frame nokey`
was measured and rejected: it returned the **next** keyframe (30.7 dB against the exact frame).

A keyframe-snapped hover decodes only one frame if it is grabbed **at the exact keyframe time**. Through today's
bucketed `GetThumbnailAsync`, the 1 s floor moves `-ss` before the keyframe again and the whole GOP is decoded. So
option (b) uses T-196's `GetExactThumbnailAsync` at the snapped keyframe time, which is why this ticket waits for
T-196.

## Recommendation

**(a) — keep the hover frame-accurate at the floored second.** The hover label names that second, and T-196
already makes cut-point pictures cheap and exact. (b) is for the owner, if they say they prefer faster 4K hover.

## Why later

It changes which frame the hover shows. That is a UX decision for the owner, not a speed fix.

## Trigger

The owner prefers faster hover on 4K over seeing the exact second.
