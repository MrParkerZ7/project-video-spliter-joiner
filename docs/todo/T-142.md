## Reported
> what is the note which sharing space on horizontal with Profiles? it's look pretty bad, you need make
> it into separate line

The "note" is the tab subtitle (*Keep the middle · drop intro (gold) & optional outro (blue) ·
stream-copy, keyframe-snapped · originals kept*) plus the gold/blue colour legend under it.

## Why this is the right fix, not just a nicer one

T-141 fixed the crash-shaped version of this: side by side, the subtitle (`*`) and the Profiles bar
(`Auto`) competed for width, the Auto column measured with infinite width and won outright, and the
subtitle's wrapping text collapsed into a sliver tall enough to push the whole tab off-screen.

That was stopped by reversing the sizing and capping the subtitle at `MaxWidth="420"`. It worked, but it
left the two elements fighting over one row and looking cramped — a cap is a crutch, and the next control
added to the bar would tighten it again.

Stacking them removes the competition instead of refereeing it: neither can starve the other at any window
width, and the `MaxWidth` crutch is gone.

## Change
The header `Grid` loses its two columns and gains a third row:

| row | content |
|---|---|
| 0 | subtitle + colour legend, full width |
| 1 | Profiles bar, full width, left-aligned |
| 2 | apply-report note (was row 1 spanning both columns) |

The bar keeps its `WrapPanel` (T-136), which now has a genuinely finite width and wraps onto a second line
when the window is narrow rather than clipping.

## Verified
Screenshotted at 1074x900 with a saved profile present — the exact width and state that produced the
original report: subtitle on one clean line, legend below it, Profiles bar on its own row wrapping to two
lines, preview + list + footer all present.

## Out of scope
The bar's internal grouping/ordering.
