## Concept

T-194's reader accepts `avc1`, `avc3`, `hvc1` and `hev1` only. All-intra MOV codecs (ProRes from cameras and editors,
MJPEG, DNxHD) usually carry no `stss`, which means every sample is a keyframe. That is easy to read, but it is
outside the verified subset.

## Why later

H.264/HEVC parity should hold in use first, and all-intra 4K sources are not what T-187 reported.

## Trigger

T-194 has shipped without a parity incident, and the owner works with ProRes or MJPEG sources.
