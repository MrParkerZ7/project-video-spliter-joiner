## Concept

`SplitEngine` pre-flights free space (`EnsureEnoughFreeSpace`, `SplitEngine.cs:176, 541`, SPEC-001 I31) and Bulk
pre-flights the batch (SPEC-002 I24-I26). `JoinEngine` has no `IDiskSpaceProbe` at all. A multi-GB 4K join can copy
for minutes and then fail at the end with `DiskFull`.

The fix:
- inject `IDiskSpaceProbe` into `JoinEngine`;
- refuse early when the sum of the input sizes exceeds the free space of the output volume (the temp file and the
  final file share one volume);
- keep the `-28`/`ENOSPC` → `DiskFull` mapping.

## Why later

It is robustness, not 4K speed, and the scenario has not been measured.

## Trigger

A report of a join that failed for space at the end. Or the next pass over Join.
