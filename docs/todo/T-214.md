## Concept

- Join's chip probe (`JoinViewModel.cs:620`, `ConfigureAwait(true)`) and the first compatibility probe of each pass
  start their ffprobe processes from the WPF UI thread. Adding many clips causes a small stutter.
- Bulk *Delete originals* (from `BulkCutViewModel.cs:322`) runs synchronously on the UI thread, with
  `Thread.Sleep` retries in `ShellRecycleBin.cs:86`.
- After a split, the recycle and empty-bin calls also run on the UI thread.

## Why later

These are not 4K-specific, and the stutter was not measured. T-193 removes most of Join's repeat probes.

## Trigger

The owner notices a freeze when adding many clips or deleting originals.
