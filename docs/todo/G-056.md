## Why

User: *"profile thumnail should also keep original image size for show on thumnail proifle tootip fill
picture"*

Measured before planning, because the wording could mean two different things. Every picture in the real
store is **16:9 and 64 or 96 pixels wide** — not one is 320:

| stored width | count |
|---|---|
| 64px | 6 |
| 96px | 4 |
| unreadable (1 byte, [[T-170]]) | 1 |

The hover card's image box is 320x180, also 16:9. So `Stretch="Uniform"` does **not** letterbox them — it
**enlarges them five times**. The card is full, and what fills it is a 64px picture blown up to 320. That
is what "keep original image size … fill picture" is describing.

## The contradiction this exposes

SPEC-007 **I107** already states the rule, for storage:

> inflating a 64px image to 320 adds bytes and no detail, turning "small but sharp" into "large and soft"
> — the outcome the preview exists to avoid.

The preview does the exact thing the storage rule forbids, at render time. Storage refuses to inflate; the
card inflates anyway.

**Decided 2026-09-13 via `todo-options`:** the card keeps filling — that is what was asked for — and a soft
picture says so. I107 is rescoped to storage; rendering may enlarge, but never silently. (The plan first
argued the other way; measuring the real library, 11 pictures all at 64–96px and none regenerable, is
what changed it.)

## The part that cannot be fixed retroactively

[[T-169]] raised the capture width to 320 but deliberately did not migrate existing pictures — that was
the fourth option, considered and deferred. These eleven are all older than that change, which is why they
are still 64-96px.

They cannot be regenerated automatically: `CutProfile` is `(Name, IntroFromStart, OutroFromEnd,
ThumbnailPath)` and records **nothing about where the picture came from**. The source video is not known,
so nothing can re-grab the frame. Only a fresh *Use current frame* or *Thumbnail…* can replace one, one
profile at a time.

## Out of scope

Changing what the chip does. At 28x28 it crops to fill (`UniformToFill`) and that is right for an icon.
