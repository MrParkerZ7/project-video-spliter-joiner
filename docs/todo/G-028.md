## Why / vision
Clicking the play/scrub bar often doesn't seek to where the user clicked — it's delayed and warps back/forward. The scrub slider drives seeking, but a track click behaves wrong.

## Grounding (2026-07-18 — root cause found)
- `PlayerView.xaml` `ScrubSlider`: `Value="{Binding PositionSeconds, TwoWay}"`, `Thumb.DragStarted/Delta/Completed` wired for live scrub (T-051) + the T-033 pop-back hold. **`IsMoveToPointEnabled` is NOT set** anywhere in `src/App` → defaults to **False**.
- With `IsMoveToPointEnabled=False`, clicking the slider TRACK does a **page-step** (`Value += LargeChange` toward the click), NOT a jump to the click point. Each step flows `Value → PositionSeconds → Position setter → BeginSeek`, so a click can fire several partial seeks stepping toward the target → "doesn't go where I click," "delay," "warp back/forward."
- The live-scrub coalescing + pop-back hold (`_isUserScrubbing`, `_pendingScrubTarget`, `BeginSeek`/`OnSeeked`) are engaged only by the THUMB drag (`OnScrubDragStarted → BeginUserScrub`); a plain track click doesn't set `_isUserScrubbing`.
- The separate `TimelineView` strip click (`OnTrackClicked`) computes X→time and calls `Player.Scrub(time)` → already jumps to the click point (likely NOT the culprit; confirm + don't regress).

## Decisions
- **D1 · `IsMoveToPointEnabled="True"` on `ScrubSlider`** — a track click then jumps the thumb straight to the click point (one `Value` change to the clicked time), so it seeks to exactly where clicked instead of page-stepping. This is the primary fix.
- **D2 · Route the click through the robust seek path cleanly** — with move-to-point on, a mouse-down on the track moves the thumb to the point and typically begins a thumb drag (so `DragStarted`/`DragCompleted` may fire). Verify a click results in **exactly one** seek to the click point, with the pop-back hold engaged (no warp, no double-seek). Handle the zero-distance "click without drag" case so the FINAL exact seek still fires (via `EndUserScrub` or the `Position`-setter `BeginSeek` — whichever path the click takes). If both a page/track-click seek AND a drag seek could fire, dedupe to one.
- **D3 · Don't regress** — thumb drag live-scrub (frame follows pin, no pop-back) and the `TimelineView` strip click must still work.

## Plan
```
T-075 set IsMoveToPointEnabled=True on the scrub slider; verify a click = one clean seek to the click point through the pop-back-guarded path (handle click-without-drag); keep drag + timeline-click working; test
```

## Risks
- Move-to-point may make a click both move-to-point AND start a drag → a double-seek or a fight with the hold. Trace the click path and ensure a single converged seek to the click point.
- Seek latency to a keyframe is inherent (small delay while the frame decodes) — that's normal; the FIX is removing the page-step multi-seek + warp, not eliminating decode latency.

## Out of scope
Re-architecting the seek state machine; frame-exact seeking (keyframe-snap preview is by design); the timeline-strip click (already correct — only confirm).
