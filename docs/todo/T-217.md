## Concept

T-195 adds `-nofind_stream_info` to grabs and the waveform for MP4/MOV only, where the identical picture and PCM were
measured. MKV and TS were not measured. TS in particular relies on stream probing to set up its decoders, and
without that a grab may fail and pay for the retry.

## Why later

There is no measurement for these containers, and the user's container is unknown.

## Trigger

The user's 4K is MKV/TS. Measure equivalence and timing on the bench first: its `mkv` fixture, and a `ts` fixture
this ticket adds to the bench matrix (T-188 builds only the shapes a now-ticket's Build log names, and `ts` is not
one of them).


## Build log


- re-validate (2026-10-05, from T-195): T-195 shipped the flag on the **waveform only** (MP4/MOV; SPEC-006 I3/I24, the internal `StreamInfoSkip` gate), not on grabs, so the Concept's "grabs and the waveform" is now just the waveform. The frame grab keeps the stream-info pass (SPEC-005 I27) because skipping it drops the format `start_time` that an input `-ss` counts from. The grab half of this ticket therefore depends on T-226's start-time gate (TS starts near 1.4 s, so a skipping TS grab would show the wrong frame and exit 0, with no retry); the waveform half extends `StreamInfoSkip.IsIsoBmffExtension` and SPEC-006 I24's retry.
