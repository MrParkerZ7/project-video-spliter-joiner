## Concept

`BulkTrimEngine` runs rows strictly one after another (`BulkTrimEngine.cs:10-15, 188`; D-004; SPEC-002 I15, asserted as
`PeakConcurrent == 1`). An Exact row is CPU-bound in its head encode and I/O-bound in its copy, so the two could
overlap across rows.

## Recommendation

**(a) — keep D-004's sequential rows.** After T-191 and T-192 a head takes about 2 s, and the gain from overlapping
it with the previous row's copy is unmeasured. Revisit only when a measured Exact batch (bench `bulk`, synthetic
fixtures) shows the encode/copy overlap is worth it.

**Built after T-192** (T-191 comes through it). Its trigger and Recommendation (a) both depend on T-191 and
T-192, and pipelining rows also rewrites the per-row progress slices and the `RowWeight` that T-191 introduces.

## Why later

- It conflicts with a recorded decision (D-004), so it needs the owner.
- The gain is unmeasured, and small after T-191 and T-192, when a head takes about 2 s.

## Trigger

- Large Exact batches stay slow after T-192.
- The owner wants to revisit D-004.
