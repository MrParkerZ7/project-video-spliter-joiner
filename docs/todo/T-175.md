- [ ] **USER_GUIDE — the three apply gestures.** These are step 2 *Set intro-end here* / *Set outro-start here*
      with **Apply to all ticked videos** on (today `:319-329`), step 5 **⧉ "all"** (`:363-369`) and step 6
      profile **⧉ Apply to selected / ⧉ Apply to all** (`:406-413`). The guide says:
  - a cut can be applied while rows are still scanning;
  - those rows read "→ snapping…" until their scan lands (under Exact cut the note stays hidden), and Run waits
    for them;
  - a pending row **is re-validated when its scan lands** (in Lossless it also snaps to its own keyframes then),
    not at the click. Today `:409-410` says every target "**re-snaps to its own keyframes and re-validates**" as if
    at once. That is no longer true for a scanning row, and under Exact nothing snaps, so reword it;
  - what each new part of the apply line means, in the wording **as shipped in T-173's apply-line table**
    (confirm it in `ApplyReportSummary`): `N waiting for their scan`, `K invalid (red when their scan finishes)`,
    `N not loaded, skipped`. Do not use D-005's `will snap when their scan finishes` or
    `not loaded yet, skipped`.

  All three gestures share one apply line: `ApplyToAll`, `ApplyProfileToSelected` and `ApplyProfileToAll` all
  set `ApplyToAllReport`, bound once at `BulkCutView.xaml:637`. Explain the parts in one place and point to it
  from the other steps. Also say that ⧉ and *Set … here* copy nothing, and write no new line, while the row copied
  from has not loaded yet. Keep the `:412` example only if it still matches the shipped line.
- [ ] **USER_GUIDE step 3 — frame thumbnails** (the guide handoff from [[T-174]]). Today `:349-350` says they
      "**update as you drag** each handle (a beat behind, once the cut settles)". Reword it to match the
      shipped behaviour:
  - **Lossless:** thumbnails follow drags once a row's scan has landed. While the row scans, its chip shows the
    muted placeholder, and the frame is grabbed once, when the scan lands.
  - **Exact cut:** every change of the requested time (once the row's file has been read), and a flip into Exact, update the chip (after the grab debounce) —
    during the scan and after it — to the frame where the cut is planned; before, it showed the nearest keyframe. A
    row's first frame otherwise arrives when its scan lands. An exact cut can fall back to a keyframe cut at run time for a source with no encoder mapping (SPEC-002 I50;
    already in `USER_GUIDE.md:615-616`), so say the chip shows the planned cut. Flipping back to Lossless while a
    row scans returns its chip to the placeholder.
  - In both modes the frame is taken at the cut time rounded down to the whole second (the thumbnail cache's
    granularity, `FfmpegThumbnailService.cs:16-18`) — do not promise frame accuracy.
  - A row whose file has not been read yet, or whose load failed, shows the placeholder. In Lossless a row that
    reads `loading…` shows the placeholder too; under Exact, a `loading…` row whose file has been read shows its cut
    frame once a cut is written or the precision flips. Do not write "while loading" as a blanket rule.
