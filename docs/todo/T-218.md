## Concept

`BulkCutViewModel.AddFilesAsync` (`:1330`) probes the added rows one after another (`:1407`) before the first preview
opens. Measured with 3 x 4K rows: all probed at 0.87 s (cold 1.22 s). The ideas:

- probe the rows concurrently behind a small gate;
- select the first row as soon as it is probed.

## Why later

After T-193 and T-194 the remaining add-rows wait is the probe gate itself (about 0.3 s per 4K H.264 row), which is
small.

## Trigger

The owner adds many 4K rows at once and finds the first preview slow to appear.
