## Concept
Make a click on the player scrub bar seek to that exact point (not a page-step toward it), landing cleanly through the existing pop-back-guarded seek path, without regressing thumb-drag live scrub or the timeline-strip click.

## Design
- **`PlayerView.xaml`:** add `IsMoveToPointEnabled="True"` to `ScrubSlider`. Now a track click sets `Value` directly to the clicked time.
- **Verify the click path in `PlayerViewModel` / `PlayerView.xaml.cs`:** with move-to-point on, a track mouse-down moves the thumb to the point and usually starts a thumb drag (`OnScrubDragStarted → BeginUserScrub`, then `OnScrubDragCompleted → EndUserScrub(final)`); a pure click may instead just change `Value` → `Position` setter → `BeginSeek`. Ensure EITHER path yields exactly ONE seek to the click point, with `_seeking` hold engaged so echoes don't warp it. Specifically:
  - Click-without-drag (DragStarted then immediate DragCompleted at the same value, or no drag events at all) must still fire the final exact seek to the click point.
  - Guard against a double-seek (a page/value-change seek AND a drag-completed seek landing back-to-back) — dedupe if the last seek target equals the new one within tolerance.
- **Confirm `TimelineView.OnTrackClicked`** still seeks to its click point (`Player.Scrub`) — don't change it unless it shares the bug.

## Acceptance criteria + Case-Coverage Matrix
- [ ] Click near the far right of the scrub bar → video seeks straight to ~that time (not one page-step).
- [ ] Click while playing → seeks to the click point and keeps playing from there; no warp back/forth.
- [ ] Click while paused → frame updates to the clicked time (allowing normal keyframe-decode latency), no bounce.
- [ ] Thumb drag live scrub still follows the pin with no pop-back (T-033/T-051 intact).
- [ ] Timeline-strip click still seeks correctly.
- [ ] A click produces exactly one converged seek (no double-seek/warp) — add/adjust a unit test on the seek path if feasible (e.g. a click-to-point drives a single `BeginSeek`/`Scrub` to the target).
- [ ] Build 0-warning; suite green.
- Matrix — Required-Success: click anywhere → seek to that point. Boundary: click at 0 / at end; click==current position (no-op or single seek); rapid clicks (each converges, last wins). Required-Fail: none.

## Dependencies
None.

## Risks
Move-to-point starting a drag → double-seek/hold fight. Trace the exact click event sequence; dedupe to one seek. Don't regress drag scrub or timeline-strip click.

## Out of scope
Seek state-machine rewrite; decode-latency elimination; timeline-strip click (confirm only).
