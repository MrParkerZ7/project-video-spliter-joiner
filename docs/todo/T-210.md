## Concept

- Concatenating 3 parts of the 4K file takes 1.37-2.25 s, against 1.30 s for one `-c copy` of the same bytes. That
  is about 1 s of per-part overhead. The suspected cause, each part's own stream-info probe, is inferred.
- `-auto_convert 0` was measured and rejected, because it changes the packet streamhash.
- The same question applies to the split's `-c copy` runs.

## Why later

These are the sacred copy runs (ADR 0001, the CLAUDE.md `-c copy` bullet). Any flag change needs streamhash
equivalence across the whole fixture matrix first, and the gain is about 1 s per join.

## Trigger

After G-059, joins of many 4K parts are still the slow step.
