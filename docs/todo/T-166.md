## Found

While updating `_index.md` for SPEC-010 I71/I72 (the [[T-154]] trace follow-up), the file's own prose
turned out to describe this gap in the past tense:

> **Keep this headline and the TOTAL row in step.** They disagreed for two days (723 vs 751) because
> `SpecIndexFreshnessTests` only parses rows whose first cell is a linked `SPEC-NNN` — the TOTAL row
> and every line of prose here are invisible to it.

So the guard is doing its job precisely and narrowly: it recounts each per-spec row against the file on
disk and fails if one drifts. It has **nothing to say** about the `**TOTAL**` row or the
`Total documented invariants: **N**` headline — and those are the two numbers a human actually reads.
The 723-vs-751 disagreement lasted two days with the suite green throughout.

The workaround today is a paragraph in `_index.md` asking whoever edits it to keep them in step by hand.
That is the same hand-maintained-copy failure mode that produced the stale picker filter ([[T-158]]),
the `_GAPS.md` header saying 9 while its list held 13, and the ROADMAP's frozen "1111 tests" — all
found and fixed in this drive. A note asking for care is not a guard.

## Design
1. Extend `SpecIndexFreshnessTests` with two assertions:
   - the `**TOTAL**` row equals the **sum of the per-spec rows** it already parses;
   - the `Total documented invariants: **N**` headline equals that same total.
2. Parse the headline by regex against the literal prose shape, and fail with the two numbers named, so
   the message says which one to fix rather than "assertion failed".

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Editing a per-spec count without updating TOTAL fails.
- [ ] Editing TOTAL without updating the headline fails.
- [ ] Mutation: changing either number alone kills a test; changing all three consistently passes.
- [ ] The "keep this in step by hand" paragraph in `_index.md` is replaced by a pointer to the guard —
      the prose should say what enforces it, not ask a human to.

## Out of scope
Re-measuring the `Covered` / `Gaps` columns, which `_index.md` already says plainly are **not**
re-measured. That is a bigger job ([[T-140]]-shaped) and independent of these two numbers agreeing.
