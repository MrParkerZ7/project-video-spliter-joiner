## Concept

T-194 removes the whole-file read for MP4/MOV only. An MKV (OBS records MKV) or TS file still pays the
`-show_packets` scan, which reads 100% of the file (2283 of 2283 MB on a 5-min 4K file). Candidates measured or
named during T-187:

- **A managed Matroska Cues reader** with the same two-window ffprobe anchor as T-194. It is fast, but Cues often
  index only cluster-start keyframes, not every keyframe. The reader would have to prove completeness or decline.
- **Windowed per-cut lookups** (`ffprobe -read_intervals <t-W>%+<2W>`): measured 0.29-0.41 s and about 77-125 MB per
  cut, whatever the file length. They break the whole-list contract the planner's average GOP, the coarse-GOP
  warning, `AddCutAt` and Bulk's `KeyframesReady` gate rely on, so three screens would need a new snap flow.
- **TS has no index.** Only a windowed lookup or the full scan applies.

**Built after T-194** (T-188 comes through it). The Cues reader reuses T-194's two-window ffprobe anchor, which
does not exist before T-194, and the owner's MKV/TS answer can fire this ticket's trigger during the drain.

## Why later

- The user's container is unknown.
- Cues completeness is unverified.
- Windowed lookups have a large blast radius.

## Trigger

- The owner says the slow 4K files are MKV or TS.
- After T-194, the bench shows MKV/TS loading as the remaining 4K wait.
