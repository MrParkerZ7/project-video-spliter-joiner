## Concept

Measured on the 4K fixture at 104.5 s with the input seek:

| encoder | head time | PSNR vs source |
|---|---|---|
| h264_nvenc p4 cq19 | 1.52 s | 45.7 dB |
| cuda decode + nvenc | 1.45 s | 45.7 dB |
| libx264 veryfast (T-192) | 2.16 s | 40.7 dB |

At 540.5 s on the 10-min file: nvenc 1.47 s against veryfast 1.99-2.21 s. hevc_nvenc: 1.25-1.64 s.

What it would take:
- per-machine detection and a software fallback;
- quality settings, because the default nvenc rate came out at about 3 Mbit/s against a 60 Mbit/s source;
- a joint proof per vendor (T-189's check);
- the head's output would depend on the machine.

## Why later

- After T-192 it saves about 0.5-1 s per row.
- It is a new subsystem with untested concat compatibility.

## Trigger

Long Exact batches still feel slow after T-192.
